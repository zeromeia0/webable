How this shared workspace works

  • Incomes are summed automatically. Each member links one of their own workspaces, and its incomes appear here as read-only entries (prefixed with the member's name).
  • Expenses are entered manually by default; that's the recommended approach for shared costs. You can also include members' own expenses below.
